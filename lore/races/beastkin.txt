Beastkin are the creations of the vile Bloody Star Cult. An amalgamation of many different animals, they are engineered to take advantage of the sinister and barbaric aspects of the 
star-god Khurgan - they have an insatiable urge to slaughter, and grow stronger when they indulge this bloodlust. They form the rank-and-file of the Bloody Star Cult's armies in 
their efforts to revive Khurgan by spilling the blood of nations.
