Beastkin are the creations of the vile Cult of the Bloody Star. An amalgamation of many different animals, they are engineered to
take advantage of the sinister and barbaric aspects of the Star-God Khurgan - they have an insatiable urge to slaughter, and 
grow stronger when they indulge this bloodlust. They form the rank-and-file of the Bloody Star Cult's armies in their efforts 
to revive Khurgan by spilling the blood of nations.
