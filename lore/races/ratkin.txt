During the Second Night, many great cities were left abandoned as their civilian populations were slain by the encroaching Darkness, yet vermin are harder to exterminate.
Exploiting the parity within the Darkness, they wished themselves greater, and metamorphosed into the Ratkin. Using the myriad of diseases they harboured as a potent weapon,
they managed to carve out many great ruin-kingdoms after the coming of the Second Age.
