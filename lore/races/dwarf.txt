The second eldest of all the races, who awakened after finding relics in the primordial ooze that made up the world before the 
birth of the Star-Gods. Using the disparity that these enigmatic objects brought into a reality that was otherwise in perfect 
parity, they laboured to create the false god Narag, who was known as the Radiant Mother. Thus began the war that would bring an end
to the First Night. The Dwarves emerged from this war sturdier than ever, yet still unable to resist blades of purest shadow.
