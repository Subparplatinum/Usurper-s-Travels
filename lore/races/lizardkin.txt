Lizardkin are the greatly diminished descendents of the Drakespawn, the children of the Star-God Drakkak. After Drakkak's banishment
in the Second Age, the other Star-Gods decided to forcefully devolve the Drakespawn, creating the Lizardkin. Ashamed of what they 
had now become, many fled out of sight and out of mind, dwelling in topical forests and swamps. They soon became masters of the 
poisonous arts to defend their new homes.
