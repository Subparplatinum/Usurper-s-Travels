After the Second Night, when the victorious star-gods left to wage their Astral War out in the void, they created another race to 
lead all mortals to a new age of prosperity. These were the Star-Elves, a race born of light who were masters of Astra's magic. 
Although they have worked tirelessly to bring about a perfect world, their cousins, Solei's Sun-Elves, have instead worked 
tirelessly to bring about their god's own twisted idea of 'perfection'.
