At times, the Darkness is forced to obey the laws of the Star-Gods, and must give form to its formless servants. 
Thus do umbral daemons clothe themselves in the primordial sludge of creation, and seep out of the lightless abyss to defile all
the light touches...
\p

Ooglesh is one of the greatest of these monstrosities - they have waged war on the lightless plains of deep Gundu Narg, in the 
under-hells of accursed Hrocharad, and even across the shining marble spires of the Pale City itself. Now they have returned from 
the Outer Void at the command of their fell master, to ensure that True Night may blight the world again.
