It was after the First Night that the Star-Gods gave chase to the banished Darkness, and appointed Solei to watch over the mortal
world in their absence. Yet much of mortalkind did not sing praises to Solei for his protection, and chose instead to glorify the 
Star-Gods whose light shone upon them from far away, who stood vigil over the Outer Void and fought the Darkness Above.
\p

Now, millenia have passed, and Solei is closer than ever to avenging this humiliation. The Star-Gods are routed, and the 
free peoples of the world fight a hopeless war against the Darkness even as the Sun-King's numberless legions sweep across their 
battered nations. 
\p

Yet there is still hope, because of you, Usurper. Your travels are at an end, and you stand at last before the Solar Throne, 
before the King of the Sun-Elves, before the very tyrant who slew your god!

Solei has no power here, not while He struggles against the Shadowy Godbeast. Now is the time to fulfill your oath. Strike down 
the Sun-King Ra'ah, and usurp his throne!
\p 
For Khurgan! For Vengeance! For the Twilight Everlasting!
