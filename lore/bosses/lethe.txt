Those who have lost their memory are plagued with visions, recurring dreams of a lightless abyss, and the nice old man who lives
within. They say he greets them like an old friend, and invites them into his home, where he says they can rest for the night. 
\p
None among the living have ever taken him up on his offer.
\p
You feel a serene sense of peace now, as the forest around you melts into black ichor. As your light begins to fade, choked by the 
inky black tendrils of True Night, you see him in the distance, calling for you to come home...
