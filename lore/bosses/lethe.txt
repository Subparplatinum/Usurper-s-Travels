Those who have lost their memory are plagued with visions, recurring dreams of a lightless abyss, and the old man who lives
within. They say he greets them like an old friend, and invites them into his home, where he says they can rest for the night. You feel
a serene sense of peace now, as the forest around you melts into black ichor. Even as your light sputters, choked by the inky
black tendrils of night, you see him in the distance.
