The final obstacle of your travels lies in a pool of his own burning blood. Ra'ah is already slain, and upon the Solar Throne sits 
one of your kindred, basking in Solei's mournful dusk-glow. In their grief, Solei flares with a light that burns the sky, boiling
oceans and setting cities aflame. Then, with the last of their power expended, they hang limply in the firmament, 
awaiting one final duel with the Darkness. As you step forward to confront the False Usurper upon your rightful throne, 
the immense black mass sweeps across the horizon at your back, ready to strike one final blow upon the Sun-God. 
There will only be enough time for one last battle before the onset of a True Night that may never end.
