In the long-lost days of the Sun-King Eryius, in the closing days of the war against the False Light, Eryius and the tattered 
remnants of his Iconoclast Legions mounted one final assault on the Divine Gateway deep below the earth. Against all odds, they 
struck down its defenders and slew the ersatz god who shone beyond. Eryius knighted all one thousand of his remaining warriors that
day, and since then the Sacred Order of Solar Knights have stood unflinchingly beside every one of Solei's Chosen. Hear now their
cry:
\p

"Et dies veniet cum exaltabitur! Et lux eius caelum comburet!"
