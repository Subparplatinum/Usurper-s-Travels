'Burning Vengeance' - the floating citadel-ship from which Raah coordinates his armies, swarms with his finest soldiers. Some of them stand before you now
 - a knight and their comrades. All of them are Sun-Elves, and their armour is decorated with ostentatious golden ornamentation that glows with their inner 
radiance. Yet their looks are deceiving, for they are servants of Solei, and their souls have been blackened by the ash of the countless innocents consumed 
in the fires of Raah's crusade.
