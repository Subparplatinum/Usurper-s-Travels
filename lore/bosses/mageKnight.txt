If there are any in the world who are strong enough to slay a Usurper, it is a Solar Knight. These Sun-elves have sworn an oath to 
cleanse the world in solar fire, and raise Solei to the highest place in the pantheon of the star-gods. It doesn't matter if they
die, or if their attrocities burn their souls black, or if the very world is destroyed by the True Night. As long as one day Solei 
will stand as the greatest of the gods because of their actions, even if it is only for a millisecond, they will do whatever must be
done to see it happen.
