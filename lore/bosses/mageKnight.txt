'Burning Vengeance' - the floating citadel-ship from which Raah coordinates his armies, swarms with his finest soldiers. Some of them stand before you now
 - a knight and their comrades. All of them are Sun-Elves, and their armour is decorated with immaculate golden ornamentation that glows with their inner 
radiance. Yet their looks are deceiving, for they are servants of Solei, and in the fires of Raah's crusade their souls have been scorched black.
