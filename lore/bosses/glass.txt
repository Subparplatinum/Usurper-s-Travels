Only the foolish and ignorant dare to eek out a living in the sinking city of Palmor. Once the crowning jewel of the Elyraxian Empire, the
arrogance of its creators caused the city's downfall. Its towering marble facades now sink into the putrid muck it was built 
attop, and in its flooded streets all manner of ruffians clash with each other and the ratkin that have claimed the city as
their own, in an endless struggle to recover ancient relics from Elyrax's height. 
\p

No relics are more sought-after than the arms and armour of the Glass Knight. Clad all in mirrors, and wielding a shard of one as a lance, he
prowls the streets of Palmor on some unknown quest. Outlandish legends state his mirrors are windows into some other plane of existence that
overlaps with ours. Many Palmorans believe this to be true, but only the foolish and ignorant dare to eek out a living in the sinking 
city of Palmor, right?
