Before her departure, the star-goddess Guijao, known as the Blossoming Radiance, planted the seed of a Greattree in the hinterlands of the Illuminated Realms. 
This tree has grown for millennia, and now towers over the tallest mountains. However, its roots have dug too greedily and too deep, and now drink from the 
abyssal sea that stagnates deep below the earth. Imbibing the prayers of the unrighteous that all inevitably fall into that sea, this Greattree, now known to celestial 
scholars as "Guijao's Curse", has begun sprouting sin-fruit. This fruit is a delicacy to the Harpies that build cities upon its branches, yet one that robs them of their 
compassion and nobility - gifting them the strength of umbral daemons in exchange.
