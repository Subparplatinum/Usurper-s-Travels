Before her departure, the star-goddess Guijao, known as the Blossoming Radiance, planted one last seed. This seed has grown for 
millennia, and is now a great tree that towers over the tallest mountains. However, its roots have dug too greedily and too deep, 
and it now drinks from the abyssal sea that stagnates in the lightless abyss below the world. To eat the fruit that grows on this 
tree grants great power, yet it also makes you a slave to Darkness. For the kingdom of Harpies who have fled to the tree's upper
branches, there is no other option.
