Before her departure to fight in the Astral War, the Star-Goddess Guijao planted one final seed. It has taken millenia for this seed
to grow into the Great Tree of Guishu that now towers above even the firmament-piercing Starsword Mountains. 
However, its continent-spanning roots have dug too greedily and too deep, and it now drinks from the abyssal sludge-sea that 
stagnates far, far below the world. Those who consume the sin-fruit that grows on this tree are granted the power of unrighteous 
prayers that float like dregs in the sludge-sea, in exchange for their possession by the servants of the Darkness Below. 
For the kingdom of Harpies who have been exiled to the tree's upper branches, there is no other option but to consume this fruit.
