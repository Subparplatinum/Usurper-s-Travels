The gateway out of the once populous, verdant land of Ziamiort, which now sprawls frozen and dead, the Silent Citadel is a bastion of vital 
importance. Its icy walls and those that man them are the only thing preventing the Cult of the Bloody Star from marauding across the lands, 
spreading yet more death and destruction in their wake. Atop the citadel's throne sits the one feared as the King of Winter - and long has he 
boasted that the sound of not one beating heart shall ever be heard within his citadel's walls. Unfortunately, Usurper, the King takes his 
boasts very seriously.
