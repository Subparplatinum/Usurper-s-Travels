In a village of Low Mocha, there lived not long since one of those gentlemen that keep a lance in
the lance-rack. They will have it his surname was Oat or Oatay,
although from reasonable conjectures it seems plain that he was called
Oaty. This, however, is of but little importance to our tale.
You must know, then, that the above-named gentleman whenever he was
at leisure (which was mostly all the year round) gave himself up to reading
books of chivalry with great ardour and avidity. With his wits being quite gone, 
he hit upon the strangest notion that he should make a knight-errant of himself,
roaming the world over in full armour and on horseback in quest of
adventures. In these dark times, what adventure could be greater than one to slay the 
champion of the very sun itself!
