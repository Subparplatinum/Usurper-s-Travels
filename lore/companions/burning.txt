Once one of the many dragonhunters that called the frigid city of Drakkengrad their home, Karl was part of an ill-fated expedition
to the far northern reaches of the realms. The only survivor of this expedition, Karl had to eat the flesh of many drakes to 
stay alive. Now he is cursed - his own flesh burns with the fires of the Star-God Drakkak, and he is
hunted by the very dragonhunters that once fought beside him. Only by doing a deed as noble as slaying the traitorous king Ra'ah
can he hope to clear his name.
