Exiled former heiress to an illustrious cartel that operates deep in the sunburnt south, Skyla has never sworn allegiance to any
deity, except the artifical god known as "profit". Yet not even that could save her, when her family schemed to have her cast out
of the business she was destined to inherit. Destiny is not easily defied, however, and now her eyes are set on Ra'ah's treasury.
When she gains access to such fabulous wealth, vengeance will no longer be out of her reach.
