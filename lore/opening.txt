For millennia, the star-gods had fought a war to defeat the Darkness. That war is now lost. 
\p

The sun-god Solei betrayed their stellar brethren, and Khurgan the Bloody Star was the first of the gods to fall.
\p

Now, Darkness stalks the realms again - the laws of reality begin to faulter, and nations are driven to the brink of annihilation by its relentless assault.
\p

Lord Raah, Chosen of Solei, has taken advantage of this chaos, leading a crusade to conquer the realms in the name of the sun-god.
\p

That is the state of our world, Usurper. I know the rite of sacrifice has taken a great toll on your body, mind and soul, but no sacrifice is in vain. Now you
may accomplish what you have given so much to do - travel the world, grow in strength, strike down that false lord Raah and take his throne. Only then, may 
we stand a chance against the Darkness. 
\p

May Khurgan bless your journey, and make the blood of your enemies flow freely!
