For millennia, the Star-Gods had fought a war to keep the Darkness at bay. That war is now lost. 
\p

The Sun-God Solei betrayed their stellar brethren, and Khurgan the Bloody Star was the first of the gods to fall.
\p

Now, True Night threatens to blanket the world again - the laws of reality begin to faulter, and countless peoples are driven to the brink of annihilation by its relentless assault.
\p

Sun-King Ra'ah, Chosen of Solei, and Khurgan's murderer, has taken advantage of this chaos, leading a crusade to conquer the world in the name of the Sun-God.
\p

Yet the free peoples of the world are not without hope, for among them walk Usurpers - great heroes who are willing to sacrifice everything so they may
save their world from the Darkness that threatens to consume it.
\p

Strengthened by Khurgan's divine energies, it is they who shall take Ra'ahs throne, enact the Bloody Star's judgement upon the Sun-God, and wage what may be the final war against the Darkness.
\p
